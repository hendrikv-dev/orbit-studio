# Product Vision

This document describes current product direction, not durable product law. The canonical product
principles are in `docs/ORBIT_CONSTITUTION.md`.

This project is the first public-facing slice of a future open-source astrodynamics platform.

The long-term goal is a modern alternative to tools like FreeFlyer, STK, and GMAT: accurate, capable, open, visually understandable, and usable without requiring scripting for common workflows.

## Current Product Focus

The current product focuses on making two experiences coherent and releasable:

### Explorer
Explore the source-limited orbital environment and its history.

Explorer should feel like a premium interactive data visualization: beautiful, minimal, futuristic,
and immediately understandable. It should answer: "What can the available evidence tell us is or was
around Earth?"

### Playground
Play with an orbit.

Playground should let users understand orbital mechanics by manipulating a simple orbit directly. It should answer: "What happens if I change this orbit?"

Future Mission Design, Scenario Analysis, Library/Encyclopedia, rockets, Hohmann transfers, lunar
missions, and Mars missions remain strategic possibilities rather than commitments of the current
release.
